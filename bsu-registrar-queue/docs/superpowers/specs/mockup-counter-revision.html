BSU Registrar Queue System — revision mockup

Two screens, two jobs

The display board is signage — read from across a waiting area, at a glance, by someone who isn't watching it closely. The counter interface is a work tool — operated all day by one staff member who needs every action within a click. The mockups below show both after the skip/recall, auto-advance, walk-in, and priority-lane additions.
